Swap neighbours to match pieces of one colour by how they move:

♜ rooks: straight lines · ♝ bishops: diagonals · ♛ queens: any line, and bridge rooks and bishops

♟ pawns: straight lines · ♞ knights: chain of three in L-jumps · ♚ kings: touching group of three

Four in a line clears the whole line. Five pawns blast 3×3. Five knights wipe that colour’s knights.
